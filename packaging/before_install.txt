PSVR Screen - your PC in the PS4 VR headset (PSVR), without SteamVR.

YOU NEED
  - A PSVR (the PS4 one) with its processor box, connected to the PC by HDMI and USB
  - Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  - A controller: a DS4 (by USB or Bluetooth) or any Xbox controller. A mouse works too.

WHAT THIS INSTALLER CAN SET UP (you choose on the next pages)

  1. PSVR Screen - the app.                                            NEEDED
     Your windows around you in VR, videos, photos, games on a big screen,
     360-degree surroundings.

  2. Virtual Display Driver - extra invisible monitors.                 NEEDED
     The "VR monitors" your windows move to while you see them in VR, and
     the sharp 4K screen. Free (MIT license), included in this installer.

  3. DS4Windows + ViGEmBus - the DS4 in non-Steam PC games.            OPTIONAL
     Not needed in VR: PSVR Screen reads the DS4 itself (buttons, sticks
     and its motion for the laser pointer). Steam games understand the DS4
     on their own too. Only for other PC games that need an Xbox controller.
     Free; downloaded from their official pages during the install.

  4. Steam + SteamVR - for real VR games.                               OPTIONAL
     PSVR Screen hands the headset to SteamVR for VR games and takes it back
     when you press "Open in VR again". Free; Steam's official installer is
     downloaded, then SteamVR is installed from Steam.

  5. iVRy - makes the PSVR work in SteamVR.                            OPTIONAL
     Needed only for VR games (with item 4). Paid add-on on Steam (there is
     a free "Lite" demo): the installer only opens its Steam page.

Nothing is installed without you ticking it. Items 2 and 3 need
administrator rights (Windows will ask).

PSVR Screen is free and open source (GNU GPL v3). The software it includes
and installs is listed in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md in its folder.
